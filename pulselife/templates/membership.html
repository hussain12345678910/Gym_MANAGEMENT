Common Questions

Can I switch plans later?

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your membership at any time through the PulseLife member app or by visiting the front desk. Changes take effect the following billing cycle.

Is there a joining fee?

New members pay a one-time registration fee of $10. This covers your access card, welcome pack, and your first fitness assessment with one of our certified trainers.
